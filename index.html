About the program

Economics field, International Business profile (code 38.03.01)

The program is designed for a wide audience of foreign applicants and provides the most up-to-date information in demand in the global educational market.

It includes economic and international disciplines aimed at developing competencies in international business, management, intercultural communication, etc.

All key subjects are taught in English.

Key disciplines

What will you learn to manage international projects

  1. 01Project Management
  2. 02Digital Economy
  3. 03Cross-cultural distinctions in business
  4. 04Russian Market & Foreign Economic Activity
  5. 05International Trade Relations (advanced level)
  6. 06Government regulation of foreign trade in Russia and China
  7. 07International monetary relations and settlements
  8. 08Foreign direct investment (Russia-China)
  9. 09Russian-Chinese foreign trade relations
  10. 10International logistics
  11. 11International marketing
  12. 12Electronic international trade

Why choose this program

  • In-depth specialization in Russian-Chinese relations

    The program is focused on gaining knowledge in the field of Russia-China relations — two key economic partners.

  • Instruction in English

    All specialized disciplines are taught in English.

  • Full migration support from the university

    The university provides full support for international students in migration matters: visa, registration, extension.
